# KlyONIX Compliance: user guide

KlyONIX Compliance keeps a company's statutory filings, licences, registrations, certificates
and legal matters on time, with reminders before anything falls due. A CA or CS firm uses it
for all its clients' companies; an organisation uses it for its own.

This guide is the same as the Help page inside the app. In the app, each answer has a
**Show me** button that opens the page and walks you through it.

## Before you start

- **Signing in.** Open your organisation's address followed by `/compliance`, for example
  `https://yourcompany.example.com/compliance`, and sign in with the email and password your
  manager set up for you. The first time, you read and accept the privacy notice.
- **On a phone.** The same address works on a phone. Add it to your home screen to use it like
  an app; what you record without a connection is sent when you are back online.
- **Finding your way.** The menu on the left (at the bottom on a phone) lists the areas you
  can use. The question mark at the top of every page replays a short tour of that page, and
  **Help** answers the questions below.
- **Searching.** Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) on any page, or choose Search in the menu, and
  type part of a name, number or reference. Every word must appear, in any order. Enter opens
  the result, or a page or action such as "New register item".
- **Notifications.** The bell shows reminders, escalations and answers to your requests. The
  same reminders also come by email when your organisation has switched email on.
- **Your look.** In **Account**, choose light or dark, an accent colour, how close together
  list rows sit, and high contrast.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Manager | Everything: sets up companies, people, reminders and the obligation library, and sees the Overview. |
| Staff | Keeps the filings, licences and legal work they are responsible for or review. |
| Auditor | Reads everything, including the reminder log, and changes nothing. |
| Client login (CA/CS firms) | Someone at a firm's client: sees their own companies' work and sends documents the firm asks for. |
| Sales and purchase users | Check a customer's or supplier's licences before a sale or purchase. |

Answers marked *Managers*, *CA/CS firms* or *Client logins* are for those people only.

## Getting started

### How do I find my way around?

The first time you sign in, or whenever you are not sure where something is.

1. The menu lists Filings, the Register, Legal work and the Calendar. On a phone it is the bar at the bottom.
2. My Work shows what you hold or review: overdue first, then this week, then later.
3. Open any row to act on it. The arrow at the top left takes you back.
4. The question mark at the top of every page replays that page's tour.

### What should I do in my first week?

*For: Managers.*

When you have just started using the app.

1. Set up each company: its kind, registrations and the people who handle its work.
2. On a firm, add your clients and link their companies.
3. Invite your colleagues from Settings, "People".
4. In Settings, choose the email account and switch reminders on.

> **Tip.** The Getting started card on My Work ticks each of these off as you do them.

### How do I see everything due this month?

Planning the month, or checking a day before you take leave.

1. Open the Calendar, and choose Month, Week or Day.
2. Each day shows its filings, expiries, legal dates and documents due. Only mine, Open only and the kinds narrow what shows.
3. Choose a day to list what falls on it, and open any of them.

> **Tip.** You can also add your work to your phone's or Outlook's calendar from Account.

### How do I get a report into Excel?

For a meeting, an auditor, or the notes to the accounts.

1. Open Reports, from the side bar or, on a phone, from Account.
2. Open a report and set its filters. It runs again as you change them.
3. Press "Download" and choose Excel or CSV.

> **Tip.** The reports are the Desk's own, so the numbers always agree.

### How do I see a list my way, and keep it?

When you look at the same few records every day, or want them as a board.

1. Narrow the list with the quick filters above it, the filter button for anything else, and the sort button.
2. Choose how to see it: as a list, as a table (its columns button picks and orders the columns) or as a Kanban board.
3. Press "Save as a view" and name it. It is under "Views" from then on; the list also opens as you last left it on this device.
4. A manager can share a view with everyone, or pin it beside the tabs.

## Filings

### How do I record a return I have filed?

Right after you file a return, payment or form on its portal.

1. Open the filing from My Work or Filings.
2. Press "Record the filing".
3. Check the date, then add the acknowledgement number and attach the acknowledgement or challan.
4. Press "Send for review", or "Mark filed" where the return needs no review.

> **Tip.** A filing sent back by the reviewer opens with your details, and their note in Remarks.

### How do I review a filing?

When a colleague has sent a filing to you for review.

1. Open Filings and choose "For review".
2. Open the filing and check the date, the acknowledgement number and the proof.
3. Press "Mark filed", or "Send back" with a note saying what to correct.

> **Tip.** Whoever prepared a filing cannot also mark it filed: a second person checks it.

### How do I record one return for many companies?

On filing day, after filing the same return for several companies.

1. Open Filings and press "File in bulk".
2. Pick the return and period, and check the date.
3. Press "Add proofs for many" and choose all the acknowledgements at once.
4. Add each company's acknowledgement number, then save them together.

> **Tip.** Name each file with the company's GSTIN or the acknowledgement number and it finds its row by itself.

### How do I add a filing that is not on the schedule?

For a revised return, a period a company was not set up for, or a filing asked for once.

1. Open Filings and press "One-off filing".
2. Choose the return from the library, the company, what it is for and when it is due.
3. It opens like any other filing, with that return's rules for proof, review and payment.

> **Tip.** A return that is not in the library is added there first, under Settings, Obligation library.

### The government extended a due date. What do I do?

*For: Managers.*

When a notification or circular moves a due date.

1. Open Filings and press "Extensions".
2. Press "Extend a due date".
3. Choose the return, the periods it covers and the new due date, and give the notification number.
4. Check how many open filings it moves, then save.

### A filing does not apply this period. What do I do?

When a company had nothing to file for a period.

1. Open the filing.
2. Open the menu at the top right and choose "Does not apply".
3. Say why. The filing is closed with your reason.

### How do I ask a client for a document?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When you need a sales register, bank statement or anything else to file.

1. Open the filing it is for.
2. Press "Ask the client", say what you need and by when.
3. The client is emailed and uploads it from their own login. You are told when it comes.

> **Tip.** Until it is accepted, change what you asked for or the date on the request's page; the client is told.

### How do I ask every client for the same document?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

Before filing day, when each client must send the same thing, such as the month's sales register.

1. Open Filings, press "File in bulk" and pick the return and period.
2. Press "Ask the clients for…", say what you need, and check how many will be asked.
3. Each filing gets its own request, needed a day before that filing is due. You are told when they have gone.

> **Tip.** Companies with a request already open, or not linked to a client, are left out. Choose a client at the top to ask only that client.

## Register

### How do I add a licence or certificate?

When you get a new licence, registration, certificate, policy or subscription.

1. Open the Register and press "New item".
2. Choose its type, company and title, and the dates it is valid.
3. Attach the certificate.

> **Tip.** Reminders go out before it expires, on the days its type sets.

### How do I renew a licence?

When an item is due for renewal.

1. Open the item.
2. Press Start renewal and, once applied, mark it applied with the reference.
3. When the new licence comes, record its validity and certificate. A manager completes the renewal.

## Legal

### A notice has arrived. What do I do?

The day a notice, order or summons comes in.

1. Open Legal and press "Log notice".
2. Give its title, kind, forum, company and the date to reply by, and attach the notice.
3. It shows in Coming up and on the Calendar until it is dealt with.

### How do I add a case that is already in court, or a trademark?

When a suit, appeal or petition is under way, or a mark has been applied for.

1. Open Legal, press "Start" and choose "Start a case" or "Add a trademark".
2. For a case: its forum, number, the date it was filed and the next hearing. For a trademark: the mark, application number, filing date and classes.
3. Its page opens; fill in the rest there as it comes.

> **Tip.** A notice that has just arrived is quicker with Log notice.

### How do I record what happened at a hearing?

After a hearing or reply date.

1. Open the case from Legal.
2. Under its dates, press Record outcome: what happened and the next date. A missed hearing takes its next date too.
3. Attach the order if there is one.

> **Tip.** A date fixed outside the app goes in with "Add a date". A date the court moves before it happens is changed with Move; one that is no longer happening, with Cancel.

### A case is decided, settled or withdrawn. How do I close it?

When the final order comes, or the matter is settled or withdrawn.

1. Open the case from Legal.
2. Press "Change status" and choose how it ended.
3. Give the date and the outcome. Any hearings or deadlines still scheduled are listed, and are cancelled when you close it.

> **Tip.** Use the same button to mark a case stayed or reserved for order, or to open it again if it is appealed or restored.

### The registry has written about a trademark. What do I record?

When an examination report, journal publication, opposition or registration certificate arrives.

1. Open the trademark from Legal.
2. Press "Change status" and choose the new stage.
3. Fill in what the registry sent: the report date, journal number and date, opposition notice, or registration number and date. The deadline that follows is added to its dates.

> **Tip.** A mark that is refused, abandoned, withdrawn or removed has its scheduled dates cancelled.

## Clients

### How do I add a client?

*For: Managers.*

When you take on a new client.

1. Open Clients and press "Add client".
2. Give the client's name, link their companies and choose the team who looks after them.
3. Add their own logins so they can see their work and send documents.

> **Tip.** Many clients at once? Press "Import from a sheet".

### How do I see one client's work only?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When you are working on one client.

1. Choose the client in the box at the top of My Work, Filings or the Register.
2. Every list then shows only that client's companies, until you choose All clients.

## Billing

### How do I set up billing my clients?

*For: Managers, CA/CS firms.*

Once, before the firm's first bill.

1. Open Settings, then Billing in its list (or Settings on the Billing page).
2. On the Firm tab give the firm's name, GST status, GSTIN, address, bank details, UPI ID, bill prefix and logo, and save.
3. On the Rate cards tab, make the firm's rate cards: a rate for filing each return, and services by the hour or item or every month, quarter or year. Give a client its card, and any own rates, on the client's form.

> **Tip.** Making bills needs the Accounts User role, which a System Manager gives in the Desk.

### How do I bill a client?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When returns are filed, services are done, or the month's recurring charges are due.

1. Open the client and press "New bill". For a client with several companies, choose the company billed: its filings, its GSTIN and its state's GST go on the bill.
2. Untick any filed return to leave for later, keep the recurring charges if they are due, add services done (hours or items) and any other lines; government fees carry no GST.
3. Press "Make the draft", check it, then "Send to client". It goes into the books and is emailed with its PDF.

> **Tip.** On the 1st of each month a draft is made for each company with something to bill; find them under Billing, Drafts. A company's own GSTIN and address go on its company's Billing tab; the client's recurring charges go on the bills of the company chosen on the client's form.

### A client has paid. How do I record it?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When money comes in against a bill.

1. Open the bill, from Billing or the client's page.
2. Press "Record payment": the amount, the date, how it was paid and its reference.
3. If the client deducted TDS, give it too; together they settle the bill.

> **Tip.** A part payment leaves the rest due. Open a payment, from the bill or Billing's Payments tab, to download its receipt, email it to the client, or cancel it if it was recorded by mistake.

### A client has paid before being billed. How do I record it?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When a client pays an advance or a retainer ahead of its bills.

1. Open Billing, then the Payments tab, and press "Record advance".
2. Choose the client and give the amount, the date, how it was paid and its reference.
3. When the client's next bill is sent, the advance is used against it; what is left stays held for later bills.

> **Tip.** The Payments tab shows every payment received, and how much of each advance is still held.

### A bill was too much or wrong. How do I credit it?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When a fee is agreed lower, a line was billed by mistake, or the whole bill was wrong.

1. Open the sent bill and press "Credit note".
2. Credit every line not yet credited, or choose one line and the amount before GST to credit on it.
3. Give the reason, which the credit note shows, and make it. What the client owes drops by the credit and its GST.

> **Tip.** Each line is credited once. A filing whose line is credited in full can go on another bill.

### A client will not pay a small balance. How do I close the bill?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When what is left on a bill will not be paid, such as bank charges the client deducted.

1. Open the bill and press "Write off".
2. Give the reason and confirm: the balance is booked as a loss and the bill is settled.

### Where does a client stand with the firm?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

Before a fee talk, a reminder call, or the year's review with the client.

1. Open the client and choose the "Billing" tab.
2. It shows what the client is expected to pay this financial year: its recurring charges for each period in the year, and each filing due in it at its rates.
3. Then what has been billed, received, deducted as TDS, credited and written off; what is still due and how late; what the client has paid ahead; and what is filed but not billed yet.

> **Tip.** Filings due this year with no rate are not counted in what is expected; give them a rate on the client's form or its rate card.

### How do I give a client a statement of account?

*For: CA/CS firms.*

When a client asks what it owes, or at the year's end.

1. Open the client, choose the "Billing" tab and press "Statement of account".
2. Choose the dates, which start as the financial year, and press "Download PDF".
3. It opens with a summary and what is still due by how late, then a section for each company with every bill, payment, TDS, credit note and write-off and the balance after each, then the bills still open, with both logos.

> **Tip.** A client with a login can download its own from its home. Its copy shows a write-off as a balance adjusted, without the firm's reason.

### How do clients get reminded of overdue bills?

*For: Managers, CA/CS firms.*

Once, when the firm wants late bills chased by email.

1. Open Billing settings, then the Reminders tab.
2. Tick "Remind clients of overdue bills" and give the days late to remind on, such as 3, 15, 30.
3. Each day, a client whose bill has reached a step is emailed it again, once per step, until it is paid.

## Setting up

### How do I stop selling to customers without a licence?

*For: Managers.*

When you may only sell to, or buy from, parties who hold a licence, such as FSSAI or a drug licence.

1. Open Settings and press "Customer and supplier licences".
2. Press "Add a rule", choose the licence, and whether customers or suppliers must hold it.
3. Choose to block the document or only warn, and the documents to check.
4. Record each customer's licence in the Register, held by that customer.

### A sale was blocked. How do I let it through?

*For: Managers.*

When a document must go ahead though the licence is missing, for instance while it is being renewed.

1. A blocked sale or purchase can still be saved as a draft; it is submitting it that is stopped.
2. Open Settings, "Customer and supplier licences", then "Blocks and warnings".
3. Find the draft under Blocked and press "Allow this once".
4. Give the reason. That draft can then be submitted.

### How do I add a colleague?

*For: Managers.*

When someone joins, or needs to use the app.

1. Open Settings and press "People".
2. Press "Invite someone", give their name and email, and choose Manager, Staff or Auditor.
3. They get an email to set a password. Where the site cannot send email yet, you are given a link to send them.

### Someone is leaving. How do I hand over their work?

*For: Managers.*

When a colleague leaves, goes on leave, or moves to other clients.

1. Open Settings, press "People" and open the person. Someone already taken off the team is listed under "No longer on the team, still holding work".
2. Under "Hand over their work", check what would move, choose who takes it, and press "Hand over".
3. Their open filings, register items, cases, trademarks, dates and document requests move, with their places as a company's default, an obligation's holder and on clients' teams. You are told when it is done; work already finished stays with them.

### How do I make sure reminders go out?

*For: Managers.*

When setting up, or if nobody is getting reminders.

1. Open Settings.
2. Under Reminders and email, choose the email account and switch reminders on.
3. Choose how many days ahead counts as due soon, and when managers are told about overdue work.

### A client says nobody reminded them. How do I check?

*For: Managers.*

When someone says they were not told a filing or renewal was due.

1. Open Settings and press "Reminder log".
2. Type part of their email address to see everything sent to them, newest first.
3. Open any of them to see the filing or item it was about.

### AOC-4 and MGT-7 have no due date. Why?

*For: Managers.*

When a company's Companies Act annual filings show no due date.

1. They fall due a set number of days after the company's AGM.
2. Open Settings, "Companies", and the company.
3. Under AGM dates press "Add an AGM", give the year's end and the date it was held, and save.

### How do I set up a company?

*For: Managers.*

For each company whose compliance you track.

1. Open Settings and press "Companies".
2. Open a company under In ERPNext, not set up yet.
3. Choose its kind, add its registrations, and the people who handle and review its work.
4. The returns it needs are added, and the calendar starts making its filings.

### A return never applies to us. How do I stop it?

*For: Managers.*

When the calendar makes filings for something you never file.

1. For one company: open the company and untick the obligation.
2. For everyone: open Settings, "Obligation library", open it and switch it off.

## Privacy

### How do I see, correct or erase my data?

When you want to know what the app holds about you, or want it changed or removed.

1. Open Account.
2. Under Your privacy press "Ask about my data", choose what you want and say what it is about.
3. A manager answers within 90 days. The answer shows under Your privacy, and comes by email when the app sends email.
4. "Withdraw consent" stops you using the app until you accept the privacy notice again.

### Someone asked about their data. How do I answer?

*For: Managers.*

When the Overview shows privacy requests to answer.

1. Open Settings, then "Privacy requests", and the request.
2. Mark it In Progress while you work on it; the page says what each kind of request needs.
3. When it is done or declined, write the response and save. They are told at once.
4. Answer within 90 days of the request.

### Personal data leaked. What do I do?

*For: Managers.*

When personal data is lost, stolen, or seen by someone who should not see it.

1. Open Settings, then "Data breaches", and press "Record a breach" at once.
2. Say when it was found, what happened and whose data it was. The page counts the hours left to tell the Data Protection Board: 72 from when it was found.
3. Tell each person affected without delay: what happened, what it means for them, and what they can do.
4. Fill in when the Board and the people were told. Until both are in, it stays on the Overview.

### How do I change the privacy notice?

*For: Managers.*

Before people start using the app, or when what you do with their data changes.

1. Open Settings and go to Privacy.
2. Fill in the grievance officer and their email: the standard notice names them.
3. To write your own, press "Write your own from the standard one", change it and save. Have a lawyer read it.
4. Everyone is asked to accept a changed notice the next time they open the app.

### Why does a PAN show as XXXXXXXX?

When a signatory's PAN or mobile number is hidden.

1. PAN and mobile numbers are personal data, so only managers see them in full.
2. Anyone else sees them masked and cannot change them. Ask a manager when you need one.

## For clients

### How do I send my firm a document they asked for?

*For: Client logins.*

When your firm emails you that they need something.

1. Open your home page; what your firm needs is at the top.
2. Open the request and upload the file from your computer or phone.
3. Your firm is told. They accept it, or send it back with a note.

> **Tip.** You can also reply to your firm's email with the file attached, from the address they have for you.

### How do I see what I owe my firm?

*For: Client logins.*

When you want your bills, or a statement for your accounts.

1. Your home page lists the bills your firm has sent you and what is still due; open one for its PDF.
2. Press "Statement of account", choose the dates and press "Download PDF" for every bill and payment between them.

### How do I see what my firm is doing for me?

*For: Client logins.*

Whenever you want to know where your filings stand.

1. Your home page lists what is open and what is overdue for each of your companies.
2. Filings, the Register and Legal list everything, filed and still to file.
